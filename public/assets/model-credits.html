Tesla Model 3

Tesla White car. by aarajesh, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Reoriented and optimized by TeslaHub; further simplified, joined and compressed with glTF-Transform. This visualization normalizes its length to 4.694 m, instances it along the conveyor and changes glass/wheel visibility at illustrative stations.

This is a Model 3 visual substitute for the Berlin Model Y reference. It is a finished-car model with limited staged visibility, not a complete manufacturing parts model.
